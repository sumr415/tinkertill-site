Delete your TinkerTill account or data

TinkerTill is made by TinkerTill. This page explains how a parent or guardian can delete their TinkerTill account, or only some of their family's data, and what happens to it.

Delete the whole account Delete only some data What's kept, and for how long

Delete your whole account

Any grown-up with a TinkerTill parent account can delete their own account. Kids don't have accounts of their own.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TinkerTill and tap the gear at the top right.
  2. Type your parent PIN.
  3. Open Family data (download or delete).
  4. Optional: tap Download our data first to keep a copy.
  5. Tap Delete my account…, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your sign-in is deleted right away, and you're signed out.

By email (if you can't open the app)

  1. Email thetinkertill@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my TinkerTill account".
  2. We may reply to confirm it's really you before deleting anything.
  3. We delete the account and tell you when it's done, within 30 days.

What deleting the account removes

If you made the family (owner)If you're the second grown-up
Your sign-in (email and password), the family, every kid profile, and all their prints, sales, orders, spools, add-ons, settings, store links and invites. The second grown-up loses access too. Your sign-in only. The family and the kids' data stay with the owner.

Delete only some of your data (and keep the account)

You don't have to delete your account to delete data. In the app:

  • One kid and everything they made: gear → parent PIN → Family data → Delete a kid's data… → pick the kid, type DELETE.
  • A saved print: My prints → Delete on that print.
  • An order: My shop → the ✕ on that order.
  • A spool or add-on: Price it → More settings → the ✕ next to it.

Deleting in the app also deletes the synced copy on every device in your family.

Or email thetinkertill@gmail.com from your sign-in email and tell us what to delete. We'll do it within 30 days.

What's kept, and for how long

DataWhat happens
BackupsDeleted data is removed from our backups within 30 days.
Things saved only on a phone or tabletStay on that device until you delete them in the app or delete the app.
Subscription purchasesApple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own rules. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
Payments made on our websitePayment records (not app data) are kept for 7 years, the longest time US tax rules usually require. Stripe also keeps its own records.
Your deletion requestA short note that the request happened (not your app data) is kept for 24 months to show we did it.

More details are in our privacy policy and terms of use.
