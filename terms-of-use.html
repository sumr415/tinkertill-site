2. Free features and the Family plan

Free, no account: the Price it calculator, including filament, add-on and cost settings, and currency.

Family plan (needs a parent account): saving prints and My prints, My shop (sales report, orders and invoices, stock), up to 3 kid profiles, a second grown-up, and syncing between devices.

Prices and free trial

  • $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year (US dollars). Prices in other countries are set by the app store and may include local taxes.
  • New families get a 1-month free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the plan you picked starts and you're charged.

Automatic renewal and cancelling

  • The Family plan renews automatically at the end of each month or year, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time:
    • iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
    • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
    • Web: use Manage subscription in TinkerTill's Grown-up settings.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. When you cancel, the plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for.

  • App Store and Google Play: Apple and Google handle your right to cancel under their own rules.
  • Web (Stripe): you normally have 14 days to cancel a purchase. Because the Family plan starts right away, at checkout we'll ask you to agree that it starts immediately and that you lose this 14-day right once it does. If you cancel within 14 days without having agreed to that, we'll refund you as your local law requires. The free trial doesn't use up your 14 days.

You can cancel any time to stop the next charge, and the plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. This doesn't take away any refund rights your local law gives you (see "Cancelling within 14 days" above), and if you think you were charged by mistake, email thetinkertill@gmail.com and we'll look into it.

When a plan ends

Nothing is deleted. Your prints, orders, sales and kid profiles are kept, but saving, My prints, My shop and syncing lock until you subscribe again. The calculator keeps working. Data from families who stop using TinkerTill is kept and deleted as our privacy policy says.
