How it works

Three steps, one little shop

Big buttons, plain words, and Undo when you change your mind. Built so a 12-year-old can use it without help.

Step 1

Price it.

Add the filament colors you used, the grams, and how long the print takes. TinkerTill adds up everything it really cost and suggests an easy-to-say price.

  • Pick a price style: Friends price, Fair price or Shop price.
  • See it in plain words: "It costs you $2.41 to make. Sell it for $6.50 to earn $4.09."
  • Selling a lot at once? Get a wholesale price that still earns money.
Step 2

Sell it.

Keep track of who ordered what, what's been made, paid for and handed over, so nothing gets forgotten.

  • Orders with a name, a note and every print in them.
  • See your sales and what you really earned, in big, simple totals.
  • Know how many of each piece you have ready on the shelf.
Step 3

Keep it.

Every print you price is saved in My prints, with its price, colors and store links. Open it again, or copy it for a new color.

  • My filament keeps your spools and their prices, so you never type them twice.
  • Track how much filament is left on each spool.
  • Add-ons like keyrings and magnets, with their own prices.
